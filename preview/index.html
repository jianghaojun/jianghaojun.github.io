About Me

I work on AI for healthcare and robotics, with a particular focus on autonomous medical robots.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Johns Hopkins University, working with Mathias Unberath and Axel Krieger on surgical automation.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at LEAPLab, Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Gao Huang. At Tsinghua, I led research on autonomous robotic ultrasonography. Our carotid ultrasound robot UltraBot appeared in Nature Communications and was highlighted by the Chief Editor of Nature Biomedical Engineering. My Ph.D. thesis received the MICCAI Best Ph.D. Thesis First Runner-Up Award and the Tsinghua Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award. Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree in Automation at Tsinghua University.
