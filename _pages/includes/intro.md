My name is Haojun Jiang (蒋昊峻). I’m currently a Ph.D. candidate at [LEAPLab](https://www.leaplab.ai/), Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. [Gao Huang](https://gaohuang-net.github.io/).

I currently lead the Autonomous Robotic Ultrasonography research (Demos for [UltraBot](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-62865-w#additional-information:~:text=First%20Demonstration%20of,with%20Plaque%20Video)/[UltraHiT](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Uj6awRDqt3LxEWV95DiftaPzehMaHMpo/view?usp=sharing)) thrust in the LEAPLab, working with lab members [Teng Wang](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=yHtp8lUAAAAJ&hl=en), [Chenxi Li](https://chx-li.github.io/), Diwen Wang and Yihang Tang, and in collaboration with [Xiangjie Yan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=MDq0cTIAAAAJ&hl=en) from [Intelligent Robotic Manipulation Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/homepageoflixiang/home) and Zhenguo Sun at [BAAI](https://www.baai.ac.cn/). Our long-term goal is to achieve intelligent and automated ultrasonography, evolving from single-organ to multi-organ examination, from standardization to personalization, and ultimately extending from examination to ultrasound-guided autonomous robotic intervention.

Recently, the carotid ultrasound robot I led, known as [UltraBot](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-62865-w), was accepted by **_Nature Communications (IF=17.2)_**! It is a great honor that this work was highlighted by the **_Chief Editor of Nature Biomedical Engineering (IF=30.4)_** [on the COVER of the October Issue](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-QUk2mCtg2XD9cWI4EuDu3L0BNR141-V/view?usp=sharing)—the ONLY one of four featured studies to be highlighted by the editor-in-chief, alongside Science and Nature Medicine articles. Moreover, I am honored to receive the _**Best PhD Thesis First Runner-Up Award**_ at the MICCAI 2025. This recognition reflects the impactful work we're doing in Robotic Ultrasonography.
